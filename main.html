Sacred Art

Prince of Peace

A painting by Akiane Kramarik

The Painting

“Prince of Peace” is a world-renowned portrait of Jesus Christ painted in 2003 by Akiane Kramarik when she was just eight years old. The artwork is celebrated for its extraordinary realism, emotional depth, and its powerful expression of peace, love, and compassion.

How It Came About

From a very young age, Akiane experienced vivid spiritual visions that inspired her to create art. She spent years searching for a face that matched what she saw in her visions.

Eventually, a traveling carpenter came to her home and became the model for the painting, allowing her to bring her vision of Jesus Christ to life.

Details Of The Artwork

The painting was completed in approximately 40 hours using oil on canvas. It is known for its luminous quality, intricate detail, and lifelike expression.

The original piece was later stolen and remained hidden for nearly 16 years before being recovered. Today, it is displayed at the Belóved Gallery in Texas.

About Akiane Kramarik

Akiane Kramarik is a child prodigy artist and poet who began painting at a very young age. Her works are often inspired by her spiritual experiences and have gained global recognition.

She has created multiple paintings of Jesus Christ, including “Father Forgive Them” and other powerful interpretations of faith and divinity.

    One God, Three Distinct Persons

    The doctrine of the Holy Trinity is the profound mystery at the very center of the Christian faith. It reveals that there is only one true God, but He exists eternally as three distinct persons: God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit.

    Distinct but Undivided: As shown in the symbol, the Father is not the Son, the Son is not the Holy Spirit, and the Holy Spirit is not the Father. They possess distinct relational roles. Yet, all three share the exact same infinite, divine essence.

    Co-equal and Co-eternal: The Father is perfectly God, the Son is perfectly God, and the Holy Spirit is perfectly God. There are not three gods, nor is God simply "changing masks." They are eternally unified in perfect love and purpose—creating, redeeming, and sustaining the universe together as One.

    The Divine Roles

    • God The Father (The Architect): The ultimate source and sovereign ruler of all creation. He is the master architect of salvation who so loved the world that He sent His only Son to redeem it. Every good and perfect gift originates from Him.
    • God The Son (The Redeemer): The Word made flesh, Jesus Christ. He stepped into human history to execute the Father’s plan of salvation. Through His sinless life, sacrificial death on the cross, and victorious resurrection, He serves as the sole mediator between God and humanity.
    • God The Holy Spirit (The Sustainer): The Comforter and active presence of God on Earth today. The Spirit dwells within believers, empowering them with spiritual gifts, convicting the world of sin, and guiding the Church into all truth.
